Care & guidance

Everything you need to know.

Looking after your piece, finding your size and choosing your diamond.

Jewellery care

Keep your piece away from perfume, lotion and household cleaners. Store it separately in a soft pouch so finer chains do not tangle, and bring it to us once a year for a gentle professional clean and a prong check.

Ask about care

Sizing guidance

Ring sizes follow the Indian scale. If you are unsure, or choosing for someone else, tell us what you know and we will guide you, including a complimentary resize within the first six months where the design allows.

Natural or lab-grown

Both are real diamonds, identical in brilliance and hardness. Natural stones are formed in the earth; lab-grown are created under the same conditions above it. The choice is yours, and we quote for either.
