At Anvesha Jewels (“Anvesha”, “we”, “our” or “us”), trust is at the heart of everything we do. We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and share your information when you visit our website, explore our collections, make a purchase or interact with our brand.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, billing and shipping address, order details and transaction information.

When you contact us, we may also collect information you voluntarily share in connection with product enquiries, bespoke jewellery, personalisation or after-sales services.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Process orders, payments, invoices and deliveries.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Facilitate custom jewellery and after-sales services.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Protect against fraud and unauthorised activity.
  • Share collection updates and promotional communications where permitted by law.
  • Meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

3. Sharing of information

We may share relevant information with trusted service providers, such as payment processors, delivery partners and technology providers, where necessary to operate our business and fulfil your requests.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect our rights, security or customers.

We do not sell your personal information as part of our ordinary business practices.

4. Data security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, maintain required business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw your consent where applicable.

You may opt out of promotional communications at any time through the available unsubscribe options or by contacting us. Essential communications relating to your orders and services may continue.

6. Third-party services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. These are governed by their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review their practices before sharing personal information with them.

7. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our business practices, services or legal requirements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated date.
