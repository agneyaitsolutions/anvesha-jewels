Privacy Policy

We respect the trust you place in us when you share your details. This page explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Only what you choose to share with us: your name, phone number and email address, along with the details of your inquiry, such as the piece, metal, diamond preference and size you are interested in. This reaches us through our inquiry form, the quote request on each piece, our sign-up, or when you message us on WhatsApp.

How we use it

  • To answer your inquiry and prepare your quote.
  • To make, deliver and look after your piece.
  • To send you news of new pieces and collections, only if you signed up for updates.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your details. We share them only where needed to complete your order, for example with a delivery partner, or where the law requires it. Messages sent on WhatsApp are also handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to look after your inquiry or order and any after-care, such as resizing.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or remove the details we hold about you, or to stop receiving updates. Just message us and we will take care of it.

Contact

For any question about your privacy, message us on WhatsApp or through our contact page.
