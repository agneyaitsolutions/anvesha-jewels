1. Product specifications

At Anvesha Jewels, we make every reasonable effort to ensure that our jewellery is accurately represented on our website. However, product images are for illustrative purposes, and photographs or screen settings may result in slight differences in the appearance or colour of a piece.

As our jewellery may involve intricate craftsmanship and handcrafted detailing, minor variations in finish, dimensions or appearance may occur. Product descriptions and measurements should be used as a guide.

We encourage you to contact us before placing an order if you require further information about a product’s dimensions, materials, gemstones or other specifications.

2. Returns & refunds

For approved change-of-mind returns, 80% of the original invoice value will be refunded, with 20% retained towards handling, insurance and logistics costs, subject to applicable law and confirmation of the applicable charges.

To be eligible, the jewellery must be unworn, unused, unaltered and returned in its original condition with the original packaging, invoice and applicable certificates.

Once your request is reviewed, we will provide instructions for returning the product. Upon receipt, our team will inspect the jewellery before processing an eligible refund.

Anvesha Jewels reserves the right to decline a change-of-mind return if the product does not meet the stated eligibility conditions, subject to applicable consumer rights.

3. Damaged, defective or incorrect products

If your jewellery arrives damaged, defective or different from the item ordered, please contact us as soon as possible, preferably within 24 hours of delivery, with your order details and clear photographs showing the issue.

Our team will review your concern and guide you through the appropriate resolution, which may include repair, replacement or refund, depending on the circumstances and applicable law.

Where a return is required, we will provide instructions for secure packaging and collection.

The recommended reporting timeframe helps us investigate promptly and does not limit any rights or remedies available under applicable law.

4. Exchanges

Anvesha Jewels offers an exchange facility for eligible purchases. Products may be exchanged at 80% of the original invoice value, subject to product inspection and verification of the original purchase.

To initiate an exchange, please contact us on +91 98986 18327 with your order details. Our team will review your request and explain the exchange process.

The jewellery must be returned in its original condition, accompanied by the original invoice, packaging and applicable certificates. Custom-made, personalised or bespoke pieces may be excluded from change-of-mind exchanges, subject to applicable law.

Any difference in value between the original purchase and the replacement product will be communicated before the exchange is confirmed.

The exchange facility is separate from any buyback programme and does not constitute a cash refund of the exchange value.

5. Important conditions

  • All return and exchange requests are subject to verification and product inspection.
  • Original invoices, certificates and product documentation must be provided where applicable.
  • Products that have been worn, altered, resized, repaired or damaged after delivery may not qualify for a change-of-mind return or exchange.
  • Nothing in this policy excludes remedies required under applicable consumer protection laws.
