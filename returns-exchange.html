Returns & Exchange

Sizing chartPrivacy policy

Every Anvesha piece is made to order, to the metal, gold purity, diamonds and size you choose. Because of that, the way we handle returns and exchanges is a little different from ready-made jewellery. Here is how it works.

Made for you

Before your piece is made, we confirm every detail with you in your quote: design, metal, purity, diamond preference and size. Please check these carefully, as your piece is crafted exactly to the confirmed quote.

Resizing

If a ring does not fit as you hoped, we offer a complimentary resize within the first six months, where the design allows. Some designs, such as eternity bands set all the way round, cannot be resized; we will tell you before you order if that applies.

Exchanges

If you would like to exchange your piece for another design, message us and we will talk you through the options available for your piece. Exchanges are assessed individually, as each piece is made to your specification.

If something is not right

If your piece arrives damaged, or differs from your confirmed quote, contact us as soon as you receive it with your order details and a few photos. We will put it right by repairing, remaking or replacing the piece.

Returns

As pieces are made to your chosen specification, made-to-order jewellery is not returnable for a refund, except where it is damaged or does not match your confirmed quote.

How to reach us

Message us on WhatsApp or through our contact page with your name, jewellery code and order details. We reply personally to every message.
