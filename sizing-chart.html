If you have a ring

You can find your ring size by measuring a ring you already own that fits comfortably. This is the most accurate way to find your size.

Lay the ring on a ruler and measure the widest distance between the inner edges, in millimetres. That is the inner diameter. Find it in the chart to see your ring size.

If you don't have a ring, wrap a strip of paper around the base of your finger, mark where it meets and measure the length. That is your circumference.

Bracelets

For line, chain and link bracelets.

If you have a bracelet

Lay a bracelet that fits you well flat along a ruler and measure its full length, clasp included. Use the chart below to find your size.

If you don't have a bracelet

Wrap a thread snugly around your wrist and cut it where the ends meet. Measure the thread on a ruler, then add 1 to 2 cm for a comfortable fit.

Bangles & cuffs

Bangle sizes follow the Indian scale, measured across the inside of the bangle.

If you have a bangle

Place a bangle that slips on comfortably over a ruler and measure straight across the inside, from edge to edge, in millimetres. Find that inner diameter in the chart below.

If you don't have a bangle

Bring your thumb and little finger together, as if putting on a bangle. Wrap a thread around the widest part of your hand, across the knuckles, and measure it. Match that to the inner circumference below.

For an open cuff, measure your wrist as for a bracelet. Most cuffs can be gently adjusted for a closer fit.
