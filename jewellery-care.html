How to care for your jewellery

  • While care has been taken to ensure that all the pieces adhere to the best quality and craftsmanship possible, the jewellery is handmade and there might be breakage, stone displacement or other issues due to conditions beyond our control. Feel free to contact us.
  • Natural skin oils affect diamonds and other precious stones and can lead to a loss of lustre. Even if you do not wear your jewellery regularly, it can still get dusty.
  • Commercially available jewellery boxes are not recommended for storage. They may have chemical adhesives that can cause discolouring and blackening.
  • When dressing, jewellery should always be put on last, as make-up, cosmetics and perfumes can damage delicate pieces.
  • Heat and light can affect a precious stone’s colour and durability. Direct sunlight can fade many stones, including amethyst and smoky quartz.
  • Gold jewellery can also darken for any of the reasons above. This is not because of inferior quality of gold.
  • Should there be any concerns, please bring the piece back to us and we will gladly rectify it for you.

How to care for diamonds and gemstones

  • To clean diamonds, use warm water, a soft toothbrush and mild soap. Do not use harsh or strong agents. Soak your jewellery in lukewarm soapy water and clean it gently with a soft-bristled toothbrush.
  • Regularly check the prongs holding diamonds and other stones, especially on rings, and make sure they are all of equal height. If one is shorter than the others, the stone might loosen and fall out.
  • Some gems can bleach in sunlight. Heat can dry out the natural moisture of some gemstones, and sudden temperature changes or extreme heat can cause fractures.
  • Do not expose gemstones to high temperatures, as this can change their colour or reduce their lustre.
  • Store your diamond and gemstone jewellery in separate boxes or wrapped in a muslin cloth, as friction between two pieces can chip the stones.
